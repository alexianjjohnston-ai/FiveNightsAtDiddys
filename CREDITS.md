## Game art and sound

Room renders, door/button/map art, static frames and most sounds come from the 2014 Java fan game `fivenightsatfreddysjava-1.0`, which uses assets from *Five Nights at Freddy's* by Scott Cawthon. `audio/power_down.wav` and `audio/chime_6am.wav` are generated placeholders (`tools/MakeAssets.java`).

Label and album names on the in-game signs refer to the artists' real music careers. All signs are drawn in code. No logos or album artwork are included.
